# Support hours and SLAs

Nimbus Desk support is staffed Monday through Friday, 08:00–18:00 US Eastern Time, excluding US federal holidays.

Severity 1 (full outage): first response within 30 minutes on Enterprise, 2 hours on Team, next business day on Starter.

Severity 2 (major feature broken): first response within 4 hours on Enterprise and Team, two business days on Starter.

Severity 3 (questions and how-to): first response within one business day on all paid plans.

After-hours coverage is available only on Enterprise as an add-on called Follow-the-Sun. It is not included on Starter or Team.

To open a ticket, email support@nimbusdesk.example or use the in-app widget. Do not send passwords or API keys in tickets.
